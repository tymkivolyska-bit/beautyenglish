At the café

October · review on 9 October

Lesson aim

Learn and practise the expressions you need at a café: getting a table, ordering, asking about the menu and paying.

What we did

  • Talked about cafés and cuisines we like
  • Matched café words to pictures: waiter, menu, bill, tip
  • Listened to a fast-food order and picked the phrases we heard
  • Practised can / could / would / is / have in café questions
  • Ordered a dialogue online and role-played waiter and guest

Key phrases

What can I get you?Are you ready to order?Could we have the bill, please?Have you got any vegan desserts?Is that eat in or takeaway?Still or sparkling water?
